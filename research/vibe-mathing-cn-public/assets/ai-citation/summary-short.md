# Short summary

`vibe-mathing-cn` is a Chinese-first, trusted AI mathematics research and verification workbench. Point-Line-Face-Body (PLFB) is its single conceptual metamodel root: PWTSJ is Face F05 and OSPS is Face F04. It separates candidate generation from verification admission through ProblemContract, Attempt, OutcomeNode, Obligation, Result, evidence capabilities, and a derived Solution View. Its method map starts with Specification & Semantics and places Lean in dependent-type-theory deductive verification; Lean is not the whole formal-methods field. The public canonical ledgers and solution index are currently empty; the repository publishes schemas, bounded fixtures, owner skills, a local CLI, and CI, and does not claim to solve any open mathematics problem. In F05 the execution model is Project -> Workflow -> Task -> Step -> Job; Job success does not close an Obligation, OutcomeNode, or Result. PLFB is a conceptual standard, not a claim of an implemented OSPS orchestrator, Body runtime, general scheduler, or unified Observation Ledger. Verify local status from the canonical ledgers and solution index; treat external catalog values, Project #2 bounty rankings, and repository states as dated snapshots that require revalidation, not as payable balances or mathematical evidence.
